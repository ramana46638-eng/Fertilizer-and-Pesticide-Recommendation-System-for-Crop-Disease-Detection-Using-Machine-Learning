About Our System

The Fertilizer and Pesticide Recommendation System is designed to support farmers by providing intelligent agricultural recommendations.

The system analyzes crop and soil information and uses Machine Learning techniques to recommend suitable fertilizers. Users can also upload crop images to identify possible diseases and receive appropriate treatment recommendations.
